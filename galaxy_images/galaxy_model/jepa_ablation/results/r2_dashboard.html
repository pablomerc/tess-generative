Reading notes:
  • Single-space runs (AION, JEPA HSC-ViT, HSC-ViT→PCA-64, Multi-survey ViT, HSC-ResNet, AE baseline, Cross-survey) have one embedding; their physics and instrument bars probe the same space. Ours, Contrastive, CF, CF-ViT, CF-Token and X-JEPA have two spaces — but note CF-Token's and X-JEPA's two spaces are heavily entangled (see REPORT.md §4c leakage table).
  • Hatched bars are variants sharing their parent run's hue, distinguished by stripe angle: of HSC-ViT — PCA-64 (45°) and Multi-survey ViT (135°); of CF — CF-ViT (45°) and CF-Token (135°); of Cross-survey — X-JEPA (45°).
  • The AE baseline is plain pixel reconstruction — not a JEPA objective; it shares the harness only.
  • Probe input width differs: 128-d for the 64-d encoders (HSC+Legacy concat), 384-d for HSC-ViT, 64-d for the PCA control (deliberately conservative), 1536-d for AION.
  • For HSC-only runs (HSC-ViT, HSC-ResNet and their PCA control), Legacy-side bars are zero-shot transfer — the encoder never saw Legacy images.
  • JEPA runs use a fixed cosine+warmup LR schedule; the published Ours/Contrastive arms carry the repo's T_max quirk. A matched-schedule contrastive control is pending before objective-level claims.
